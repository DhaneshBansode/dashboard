# 📊 Dhanesh E-Commerce Sales Dashboard  

This project presents an **interactive Power BI dashboard** analyzing sales performance of an e-commerce business. The dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue, customer behavior, product categories, and profitability.

## 📌 Dashboard Features  

### 1. **Sales by State**  
- Shows revenue distribution across different states.  
- In this dataset, **Bihar** contributes the entire sales amount.  

### 2. **Sales by Customer Name**  
- Identifies top customers driving revenue.  
- **Sonal** is the highest contributor, followed by Kartikay and Yogesh.  

### 3. **Quantity by Payment Mode**  
- Distribution of transactions across payment methods:  
  - **Cash on Delivery (COD):** 46%  
  - **Debit Card:** 24%  
  - **UPI:** 20%  
  - **Credit Card:** 7%  

### 4. **Quantity by Category**  
- Breaks down sales into categories:  
  - **Clothing:** 63% (largest share)  
  - **Electronics:** 26%  
  - **Others** contribute smaller proportions.  

### 5. **Profit by Month**  
- Tracks profitability trends over Q1:  
  - Minimal profit in **January**  
  - Significant growth in **February and March**  

### 6. **Profit by Sub-Category**  
- Analyzes which products contribute most to profits:  
  - **Accessories** and **Sarees** generate the highest profit.  
  - Other categories (Phones, Handkerchiefs, Shirts) contribute less.  

---

## 🎯 Business Insights  
- Reliance on **Cash on Delivery (46%)** suggests opportunities to promote digital payments.  
- **Clothing dominates sales (63%)**, indicating strong demand in this category.  
- **Accessories & Sarees** are the most profitable, signaling potential focus areas for growth.  
- Sales and profits improved steadily from January to March, showing a positive business trend.  
- A few **key customers drive majority of revenue**, suggesting need for customer retention strategies.
